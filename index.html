The Bonus Feature

Land 6 or more hard hats to start the free spins. It triggers ….

  • 🦺 Start with 6 free spins; collect 3+ hats in a spin to win +1 spin.
  • 🏚️ Hats build houses on the grid: straw → stick → brick. Bigger houses are worth more.
  • 🐺 At the end, the wolf huffs every house down to reveal a cash prize.
  • 🏰 Stack 3+ brick houses to fire the Mansion Jackpot.
